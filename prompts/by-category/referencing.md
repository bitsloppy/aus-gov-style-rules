# AGSM Referencing — System Prompt Drop-in

> Compiled from stylemanual.gov.au · 2026-09-22

---

## Author-date style — in-text citations

- Format: **author year** — no comma between them.
  - *Jones (2017) reported similar results.*
  - *Other results were similar (Jones 2017).*
- Two authors: use **and** not &.
  - ✅ *Black and Jones (2017)* / *(Black and Jones 2017)*
  - ❌ *Black & Jones (2017)*
- Three or more authors: **et al.** (not italicised) after the first author name.
  - ✅ *Holmes et al. (2019)* / *(Holmes et al. 2019)*
- Do NOT list all authors in in-text citations for 3+ author works.
- Same author, same year: add a lowercase letter — **(Dobell 2018a, 2018b)**.
- No date: use **n.d.** (with full stops) — *(Jones n.d.)*.
- Multiple works in one citation: separate with semicolons in **alphabetical order** — *(Abaza 2019; Pratt 2013; White and Jones 2017)*.
- Organisation authors: use the recognised shortened form in-text.
- Page numbers: only for **direct quotations**, using a colon — *(White and Jones 2019:23-24)*.

## Author-date style — reference list

- Heading: **References** (not Bibliography, Sources, or Works Cited).
- Name format: **family name initials** — no comma between name and initials, no full stops in initials. Example: **Smith AB**.
- List ALL authors — no *et al.* in the reference list.
- Publication date in parentheses, immediately after the author name.
- Italicise titles of complete, standalone works (books, reports, journal names).
- Titles of parts of publications (articles, chapters, poems): single quotation marks, not italicised.
- Online works: hyperlink the title; include an **accessed [date]** at the end.
- Newspaper articles, blog posts, social media posts: include full date — **(24 May 2018)** not just the year.
- Element order: author → date → title → publisher details → accessed date.

## Documentary-note style

- Reference markers: numbers (not symbols) in most content; symbols only where numbers could be confused with exponents.
- Symbols order when used: \*, †, ‡, §, # (double them after five: \*\*, ††, etc.)
- Place reference markers **after** sentence punctuation, not before.
- Multiple citations at the same point: one marker, all citations in one note separated by semicolons.
- Author name format in notes: **initials before family name** — **AB Smith** (opposite to reference list).
- Full citation element order: author → title → publisher → date → accessed date.
- Subsequent citations: **family name + short title** only — not *ibid.*, *op. cit.*, or *loc. cit.*
- Section heading for digital content: **Notes** (not Footnotes, Endnotes, or References).

## Legal citations

- Cite the legislation title first, then the specific section.
- Use the authorised title from the Federal Register of Legislation (or relevant state database).
- Separate reference lists: **Legislation** and **Legal cases** — alphabetical within each section.
- Reference list: roman (non-italic) type for Acts and case names in lists (even though they are italicised in body text).

## Shortened forms

- English citation abbreviations have no full stops: **vol**, **edn**, **ed**, **ch**, **p**, **pp**.
- Latin shortened forms retain full stops: **et al.**, **c.**, **cf.**, **et seq.**, **n.d.**
- Do NOT use **ibid.**, **op. cit.**, **loc. cit.**, or **id.** unless a specific publisher explicitly requires them.
- All shortened forms: roman (non-italic) type, starting with lowercase.
