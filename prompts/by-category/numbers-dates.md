# AGSM Numbers and Dates — System Prompt Drop-in

> Compiled from stylemanual.gov.au · 2026-09-22

---

## Numbers — numerals vs words

- Use **numerals for 2 and above**. Write **zero** and **one** as words.
- Always use numerals when a number accompanies a unit of measurement: **1 km**, **5 tonnes**, **0 degrees**.
- Never start a sentence with a numeral — reword or spell the number out.
- Large round numbers: combine numeral + word — **2.5 million**, **$50 billion**.
- Fractions in general text: words — **two-thirds**, **three-quarters**. Use decimals when precision matters.
- Decimals less than 1 must include a leading zero: **0.59** not .59.
- Decimal separator is a full stop, not a comma: **2.5687** not 2,5687.
- Group four-or-more-digit numbers with commas: **6,500**, **745,902,350**. No spaces.
- Consistent format when comparing: don't mix words and numerals in the same comparison.

## Ordinals

- Spell out **first** to **ninth**; use numerals from **10th** onwards.
- Centuries always use numerals: **1st century**, **9th century** — not *first century*.
- No ordinal suffix in full date expressions: **12 February** not 12th February.
- No superscript on ordinal suffixes.
- Roman numerals for monarchs and family sequences: **Elizabeth I**, **Henry VIII**.

## Dates

- Format: **21 September 2026** — day (numeral), month (spelled in full), year (four digits). No commas.
- No ordinal suffixes with months: **1 May** not 1st May.
- Abbreviate months only in tables, charts, or social media — not in body text.
- All-numeric dates (tables only): **d/m/yyyy** order — **4/6/2021** (Australian, not US).
- Financial years: **2021–22** (en dash, four-digit/two-digit, unspaced). Century spans: **1999–2000**.
- Year spans in body text: **from 2015 to 2019** or **between 2015 and 2019** — not 2015–2019.
- Years: always four digits — **1945**, **2020**. Never abbreviate ('45).
- Decades: **1980s** not 1980's.

## Time

- Separator: colon — **8:30 am** not 8.30 am.
- **am** and **pm** in lowercase, with a space before: **10 am**, **6:30 pm**.
- Don't combine am/pm with morning/afternoon/evening.
- **12 am** and **12 pm** are ambiguous — use **noon**, **midday**, or **midnight**.
- 24-hour clock: four digits with a leading zero — **07:38** not 7:38. No am/pm suffix.

## Currency

- No space between symbol and numeral: **$27.99** not $ 27.99.
- Two decimal places for cents: **$0.50** not $0.5.
- Where Australian dollars may be confused with another currency: use **A$** (no space).
- Don't combine a currency symbol and an IBAN code — choose one.
- Approximate amounts in words: **thousands of dollars** not $thousands.

## Percentages

- In text: numeral immediately followed by %: **15%** (no space).
- Written out: **per cent** — two words. Not *percent*.
- Don't start a sentence with a percentage numeral and symbol.
- Use **percentage** as the noun form — not *per cent* as a standalone noun.
- Decimals only alongside the % symbol — not fractions: **0.25%** not ¼%.

## Measurement

- Metric (SI) units only — no imperial units in current Australian content.
- Space between numeral and unit symbol: **56 km** not 56km.
- No plural s on unit symbols: **km** serves as singular and plural — not kms.
- No full stop after unit symbols mid-sentence.
- Don't mix unit symbols with words: **square kilometres** or **km²** — not *square km*.
- Spell out unfamiliar units on first use: **decibels (dB)**; then dB alone.

## Phone numbers

- Australian landlines: **02 5550 4321** (2-digit area code, space, 4+4 digits).
- Australian mobiles: **0491 570 159** (4+3+3 format).
- International: **+61 2 5550 4321** (replace leading zero with +61).
- Digits only — no phonewords (not 13 VETS).
